Before you begin

The camera is processed on this device. Video frames are analysed locally, including in the optional ML method. Model and runtime files are retrieved from the same website if installed; video frames are not uploaded. Test events are saved locally until you delete them or your browser clears storage. A voluntary export creates a JSON report you choose whether to share.

Research operator: Sammy Chehade. Contact and withdrawal: reply to the person who shared this test using the same channel and provide the record ID; they must forward your request to the operator. Deleting a local record does not delete copies already exported or sent.

Privacy: exports are pseudonymous, not anonymous. A report contains a random ID, coarse device category, your chosen role, test events, optional feedback and timestamps. If you send it using email or messaging, its recipient may associate it with you. Do not include names, contact details or sensitive information in the feedback. Provisional retention: the operator must delete received research exports within 30 days after the pilot ends. This policy requires operator confirmation before public recruitment.

Researcher-only self-labelled diagnostic. This is not an accuracy certificate. The tool stores counts and timing, never video frames. Benchmark mode is accessible to anyone with the URL.

Hand ML is not an object-tracking comparator and must not be pooled with object results. For an absent-object trial, move the object completely out of view after calibration.
