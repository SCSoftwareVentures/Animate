# ANIMATE — pre-registered demand and acquisition test (NOT EXECUTED)

**Status: PROPOSED ONLY.** No customers contacted, independent users recruited, price commitments obtained, signed buyer offers, real iPhone tests or privacy approvals. This is NOT product-market-fit evidence. Before any participation, owner must approve recruitment, exact build, consent/retention notice, data handling and participant outreach separately.

## Two independent hypotheses

**End-user hypothesis (unverified):** Independent adult educators offering one-to-one/small-group fraction lessons may benefit from a three-zone, camera-controlled 1/3–2/3–3/3 demonstration. ANIMATE does not supply general object recognition or a full teaching product; compare specifically with a *touch-based or physical teaching method*, not an invented inferior competitor. Exclude minors and classroom personal data from the initial research.

**Software buyer hypothesis (unverified):** A qualified educational-interactive studio may value transferable, privacy-conscious browser zone-control source code. Demand from teachers does not establish willingness by software businesses to buy ANIMATE IP; test separately.

## Protocol to register before seeing results

**Sample targets (planning, not statistical power):** 12 independently recruited, consenting adult educators; separately, 6 qualified acquirer decision-makers. Avoid the owner's family/friends or people financially interested in ANIMATE. Record refusals, exits and excluded trials in denominators. Recruitment and purchase discussions are NOT authorised by this plan.

**Problem discovery:** Before showing any working demo, record current lesson/task, frequency/month, existing alternative, elapsed effort, frustration/failure cost, accessibility and setup constraints. Do not ask leading “is this amazing?” questions. Prioritise a recurring, measurable pain rather than novelty.

**Comparable technical trial (requires approved physical Safari preview first):** For each educator, compare the SAME predeclared fraction task using ANIMATE and their existing approach in alternated order. Record attempted, completed, false zone triggers, elapsed seconds, setup friction and spontaneous preference. Use the exact source SHA, device/browser, light and task, include zero-completion attempts, and never infer camera classification accuracy from a user's positive impressions.

**Commercial signal:** State one specific deliverable, an exact price and actual constraints. Separate hypothetical willingness to pay from verifiable commitment (authorised paid order, signed conditional pilot, procurement approval). Never collect or spend money as a consequence of this document. For acquisition buyers classify (a) rejection/no response, (b) curiosity, (c) technically qualified request for diligence, (d) specific written acquisition terms/offer. Only (d) supports a claim of acquisition intent.

**Conservative screening gate (heuristic only):** Continue *researching* the original use-case only if ≥8/12 independently report a recurring material problem AND ≥6/12 show measurable improvement without unacceptable extra setup or accessibility obstacles in a fair trial. Otherwise narrow, redesign or stop. Even if both conditions hold, do not call the idea commercially validated without independently verified actual commitments. No price estimate or probability of easy sale can be inferred from this small sample.
